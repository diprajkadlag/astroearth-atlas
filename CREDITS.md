# Credits

Every file in `public/textures`, `public/fonts` and `public/data` has a row in
the table below: its source, its license and the credit line to show. A unit
test (`tests/credits.test.ts`) fails when a row is missing.

Rules for new assets:

- Use only public-domain or openly licensed sources, and record them here in
  the same commit that adds the file.
- Do not use the three.js example textures (no stated license), Gaia-based
  star maps (non-commercial license) or the HYG database (share-alike).
- Do not show the NASA logo, and do not imply endorsement by NASA.
