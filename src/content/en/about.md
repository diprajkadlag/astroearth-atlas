# About AstroEarth Atlas

AstroEarth Atlas shows the sidereal sky beside world history, from 1900 to
2050. It is a free, open-source hobby project.

## What you can do

- Move the date with the ruler. The nakshatra ring, the Earth and the
  planetary time waves follow it.
- Read the sky table: the sign, degree, nakshatra and pada of the 9 grahas, in
  the Lahiri sidereal zodiac.
- Open world events, eclipses and the conjunctions of Jupiter and Saturn on
  the timeline. Each card gives the facts of the event.
- See the Koorma Chakra of the Brihat Samhita on the globe: in its classical
  form around Mathura, or as a modern extension over half of the Earth.
- Compare the great conjunctions from 1000 to 2100 in the sidereal and the
  tropical zodiac.

## What it is not

AstroEarth Atlas shows calculated sky positions and traditional Jyotish rules.
It does not predict events, and it is not advice of any kind. The event texts
state dated facts. They do not link an event to the sky.

## How it is made

- The positions come from astronomy-engine (MIT license). They were checked
  against NASA JPL Horizons and against Drik Panchang. The
  [method page](method.html) states every convention.
- Each world event was checked against Wikidata or an official page. Every
  summary is written in own words.
- The Earth images come from NASA. The fonts use the SIL Open Font License.
  See the [credits](credits.html).
- The site loads nothing from other servers, sets no cookies and has no
  analytics. See the [privacy page](privacy.html).
