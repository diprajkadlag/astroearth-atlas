# Privacy

Version of 11 October 2026.

## Who is responsible

AstroEarth Atlas is a private, non-commercial hobby project of the owner of the
GitHub account [diprajkadlag](https://github.com/diprajkadlag). You can reach
the owner through GitHub, for example with an issue in the
[repository](https://github.com/diprajkadlag/astroearth-atlas/issues).

## What this site does not do

- It sets no cookies and stores nothing in your browser.
- It has no accounts, no analytics and no advertising.
- It loads no fonts, scripts, images or data from other servers. Everything
  comes from this site.
- The share link keeps your view settings, such as the date and the zoom, in
  the part of the address after "#". Browsers do not send that part to any
  server.

## Hosting by GitHub Pages

This site is hosted by GitHub Pages. GitHub's privacy statement names GitHub,
Inc. (88 Colin P. Kelly Jr. St., San Francisco, CA 94107, USA) or GitHub B.V.
(Prins Bernhardplein 200, Amsterdam, Netherlands) as the company that
processes the data. When you open the site, your browser connects to GitHub's
servers and sends technical data, among them your IP address. GitHub states
that it logs and stores the IP address of every visitor of a GitHub Pages site
for security purposes
([About GitHub Pages](https://docs.github.com/en/pages/getting-started-with-github-pages/about-github-pages)).
GitHub does not state how long it keeps these logs.

The legal basis is Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR: the legitimate interest in delivering
this site safely. GitHub may process the data in the USA. GitHub states that it
has certified its adherence to the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework Principles
([GitHub General Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement)).

## Contact through GitHub

If you write to the owner through GitHub, GitHub stores your message under its
own privacy statement. Issues and their comments are public, so put in them
only what your request needs. The owner uses your message only to answer you.
The legal basis is Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR.

An issue and its comments stay in the repository until the owner deletes
them. On request, the owner deletes your issue or comment.

## Links to other sites

Event cards link to Wikidata and to official pages. Those sites receive data
from your browser only when you open such a link. Their own privacy statements
apply.

## Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right of access (Art. 15), rectification
(Art. 16), erasure (Art. 17), restriction of processing (Art. 18) and data
portability (Art. 20). You can also lodge a complaint with a data protection
supervisory authority (Art. 77).

## Right to object

Where data is processed on the basis of Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR, you have the right
to object to this processing at any time, on grounds relating to your
particular situation (Art. 21 GDPR). Send your objection to the owner through
GitHub, as described above.
